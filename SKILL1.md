Lesson Plan — 20-Lesson Investor-Grade English Curriculum
Reference file for the professional-english-lesson skill. When the user asks to "generate lesson N", take the topic and targets from the matching row below. Lessons 1 and 2 are already generated. Each lesson uses: 2 phrasal verbs, 2 professional phrases, 1 core expression, 1 idiom (the idiom column is empty for Lesson 1, which was generated with five targets).
Default audience throughout: energy-sector investor relations professionals, level C1/C1+, 2 hours, fixed nine-section structure from lesson-template.md.
Part 1 — Communicating the investment proposition
#	Lesson theme	Phrasal verbs	Two professional phrases	Core expression	Idiom
1	Presenting the investment case	build on, set out	"Our investment case rests on…" / "What sets us apart is…"	Compelling value proposition	—
2	Explaining financial performance	drive up, weigh on	"The key drivers behind this are…" / "This needs to be viewed in the context of…"	Underlying performance	The bottom line
3	Capital allocation and investment priorities	scale up, free up	"We remain disciplined in our approach to…" / "Our priority is to strike a balance between…"	Capital discipline	Put all your eggs in one basket
4	Explaining shareholder returns	pay out, reinvest in	"Our commitment to shareholders remains…" / "We aim to deliver sustainable returns through…"	Shareholder value creation	Strike a balance
5	Explaining competitive advantages	leverage, build on	"Our competitive advantage lies in…" / "This puts us in a strong position to…"	Competitive edge	Stand the test of time
Part 2 — Handling challenging investor questions
#	Lesson theme	Phrasal verbs	Two professional phrases	Core expression	Idiom
6	Responding to investor scepticism	push back on, back up	"I understand the concern, but I would distinguish between…" / "The evidence we have suggests…"	Evidence-based argument	Hold up under scrutiny
7	Explaining risks without undermining confidence	factor in, hedge against	"We have taken this risk into account…" / "Our exposure to this is relatively limited…"	Downside protection	Weather the storm
8	Discussing uncertainty and forecasts	play out, depend on	"Under our base-case scenario…" / "There is a degree of uncertainty surrounding…"	Sensitivity analysis	A moving target
9	Defending difficult strategic decisions	weigh up, rule out	"There are several trade-offs to consider…" / "The rationale behind this decision is…"	Strategic rationale	The bigger picture
10	Answering questions you cannot fully address	follow up on, disclose	"What I can say at this stage is…" / "It would be premature to draw conclusions about…"	Forward-looking statements	Put your cards on the table
Part 3 — Energy transition, sustainability and investment
#	Lesson theme	Phrasal verbs	Two professional phrases	Core expression	Idiom
11	Explaining the energy transition	phase out, ramp up	"We are taking a pragmatic approach to…" / "The pace of transition will depend on…"	Transition pathway	A balancing act
12	Balancing profitability and sustainability	trade off, align with	"These objectives are not mutually exclusive…" / "We need to reconcile short-term returns with…"	Long-term value creation	The best of both worlds
13	Presenting ESG performance	follow through on, measure against	"We are making measurable progress towards…" / "Our performance should be assessed against…"	Measurable commitments	Walk the talk
14	Explaining investment in renewable energy	scale up, bring on stream	"We are prioritising projects that meet…" / "The expected returns are underpinned by…"	Risk-adjusted returns	Bear fruit
15	Discussing energy-market volatility	pass through, offset	"We are well positioned to navigate…" / "The impact will largely depend on…"	Market exposure	Ride out the storm
Part 4 — Advanced investor communication and negotiation
#	Lesson theme	Phrasal verbs	Two professional phrases	Core expression	Idiom
16	Presenting complex financial information clearly	break down, walk through	"Let me put those figures into perspective…" / "The key takeaway from these results is…"	Material impact	Put things into perspective
17	Managing expectations	factor in, revise down	"Our guidance reflects the current outlook…" / "We would caution against extrapolating…"	Prudent assumptions	Err on the side of caution
18	Discussing investment opportunities	buy into, enter into	"The opportunity is particularly attractive given…" / "We see considerable potential in…"	Investment thesis	A window of opportunity
19	Handling disagreements with institutional investors	challenge, push back on	"I would respectfully challenge that assumption…" / "There is an important distinction to be made between…"	Constructive challenge	See eye to eye
20	Delivering a convincing investor presentation	set out, follow through on	"Let me bring this back to our investment case…" / "What ultimately underpins our confidence is…"	Credible investment narrative	The proof is in the pudding
